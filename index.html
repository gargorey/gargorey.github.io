Farha: event entertainment booking

A bilingual (Arabic-first) platform for booking magicians, hosts, mascot characters and shows for events in Saudi Arabia. Three connected parts in one codebase: the customer website, an operations dashboard, and the backend.

Happy Puzzles: a puzzle game for kids

A drag-and-drop jigsaw game for young children, with 20 original illustrations and a mode that turns a family photo into a puzzle. Fully offline, in English and Arabic.

  • Seven difficulty levels, from 2 to 25 pieces, with snap, sparkle and gentle wrong-move feedback.
  • Photo puzzles are cropped and cut on the device in a background isolate. No storage permission, no ads, no analytics.
  • Parent area behind a grown-up check, progress tracking, and unit and flow tests for the puzzle engine.
